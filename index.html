Rating a peer

peer_appraisal.html · same link, different form

You never pick who to rate

Your supervisor sets who you rate and sends each one as its own task — the link inside already knows who you're rating and which cycle, so there's no dropdown to fill in. Open it and you're straight into the questions.

Your rating is anonymous. Pooled results stay hidden until at least 2 peers have responded, so no single rating can be traced back to you. Rate honestly.
This banner sits at the top of the form the whole time you're filling it in
One person, one task. If you're asked to rate two people in the same cycle, you'll get two separate tasks with two separate links — never one task where you have to choose.

Compare where they are now to the next level

For each value, you're not just picking a number in the abstract — you're comparing what you've actually seen against what "now" looks like versus what "next level" looks like, then rating 1 to 5.

If you're ever on a PIP

Performance Improvement Plan — built with you, not just about you

What it actually is

A PIP is a time-boxed plan for closing a specific gap, written together with your supervisor. It names what's below the bar, what "good" looks like, and what help you get to close the gap. It's not a formality before an exit — most PIPs are opened to fix something specific and closed once it's fixed.

What to expect

  1. Your supervisor opens it and fills in the gaps, targets, and what support you'll get — you talk through it together, it isn't handed to you finished.
  2. You'll have scheduled check-ins over the plan's window — usually every couple of weeks — where progress gets logged honestly, not just at the end.
  3. At the review date, the result is Pass or Not Met, based on whether the targets you agreed on were actually hit.

A few things people ask
I lost the link — now what?

Check your GoodDay My Work — the original task is still there with the link in it. If you can't find the task either, ask your supervisor to resend it.

Can I go back and change something after I lock?

Not on the KPI self-review — locking is final for your side. On Peer Appraisal, once you submit, that's it too. Take a minute to read back through before either one.

Is my peer rating really anonymous?

Yes. A pooled scorecard only appears once at least two peers have rated the same person in the same cycle, so a single rating can never be worked out from the result.

What if I don't know my growth focus yet?

Write down whatever's true, even if it's small. Leaving it blank isn't neutral — it's noticed as part of the review.

Where's the exact rubric wording for my level?

Every form has a "View full rubric" link — the same reference table the forms actually score against, for every level.
